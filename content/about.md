I'm stefansko, just a regular dude working on software 9-5. I never considered blogging something I'd do, and I've never seen myself as much of a writer, so some of this might read a bit rough (while I *do* use AI for brainstorming and spellchecking, I try to keep it out of my core writing process as much as possible, so it doesn't interfere with my own thoughts).

The current turbulent changes in our field pushed me into writing down my thoughts to bring some structure to them. Forcing myself to put them into words helps me clarify them. In that regard, this is mostly for me. I don't care much about an audience. I'm putting it up here in case someone else finds it useful. If so, great. If not, that's fine as well.
